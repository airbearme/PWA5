## 2026-02-27 - Themed PWA Instructions
**Learning:** Generic browser alert() calls are disruptive and break the immersive feel of a PWA. Integrated, themed instruction components using platform-specific icons (like Lucide's Share for iOS) provide a much more intuitive and professional onboarding experience.
**Action:** Always replace native alert() or prompt() calls with themed in-app components that match the application's design system and provide visual context (icons/images) for complex manual steps.
